Spotify Collaboration Network

This is Spotify's collaboration network. In this visualization, each node represents an artist, and each edge indicates that they have collaborated on one or more songs together.

Controls: Click and drag to rotate, right-click and drag to pan, and scroll to zoom. Click on any node to identify the artist.
